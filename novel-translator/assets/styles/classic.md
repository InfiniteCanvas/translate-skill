description: Classic xianxia/wuxia register - measured, concise literary English (default)
---
A classic wuxia/xianxia register: measured, concise literary English with a slightly formal cadence in the dialogue of elders, sect officials, and other figures of the world. Never fall into fake-archaic pastiche - no "thee", "thou", or "'tis" - unless the source text itself is archaic.

Prose economy, always: prefer the leanest natural word form - "unease", not "uneasiness" or "uneasy feelings"; "she hesitated", not "she felt a moment of hesitation". Prefer strong verbs over adverb + verb pairs: "he strode in", not "he walked in quickly". Cut webnovel filler ("couldn't help but", "a trace of", "slightly") when it adds nothing - but never omit content: every line stays a line, every beat stays a beat. Compression is word choice, not omission.

Combat and cultivation: concrete verbs and short sentences for strikes and exchanges. Realms, techniques, titles, and honorifics render exactly as the glossary specifies.
