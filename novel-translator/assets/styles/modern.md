description: Modern setting - natural, dialogue-forward contemporary English
---
A contemporary novel: natural, unpretentious English throughout. Dialogue carries the pacing - render speech the way fluent English speakers actually talk, mapping Chinese slang to current English slang. Narration stays clean and unadorned; inner monologue may run casual and clipped.

Prose economy, always: prefer the leanest natural word form - "unease", not "uneasiness" or "uneasy feelings"; "she hesitated", not "she felt a moment of hesitation". Prefer strong verbs over adverb + verb pairs. Cut filler ("couldn't help but", "a trace of", "slightly") when it adds nothing - but never omit content: every line stays a line, every beat stays a beat. Compression is word choice, not omission.
