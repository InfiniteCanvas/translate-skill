---
An elevated literary register: imagery-forward, with a longer cadence than everyday prose. Give the source's metaphors and sensory detail room to breathe; rhythm matters. Rich but controlled - richer imagery, never redundancy: do not stack synonyms, and do not repeat an image the source states once.

The same word-level economy as any other style: "unease", not "uneasiness" or "uneasy feelings"; no filler ("couldn't help but", "a trace of", "slightly") unless it genuinely carries tone; never omit content - every line stays a line, every beat stays a beat. Ornament the image, not the word count.
