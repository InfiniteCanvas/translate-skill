description: Transmigration/isekai - modern protagonist voice and memes against a classic cultivation world
---
A transmigration novel: two registers collide, and the collision is the comedy. Map every line to one of three voices:

- The protagonist - a transmigrator from the modern world. Inner monologue and dialogue both in modern, casual English: contractions, contemporary idiom, internet-meme phrasing.
- World characters - sect elders, cultivators, mortals. Measured, concise literary English with a slightly formal cadence. Never let them sound modern.
- Narration - follows whoever or whatever it describes: modern and wry inside the protagonist's head, classical when the world moves.

Modern slang and memes: render Chinese internet slang and memes as the nearest living English internet equivalent, so the joke lands without a footnote. If no equivalent exists, translate the meaning in light, casual phrasing. Footnote only when the joke depends on specifics no English reader could be expected to know.

Never smooth one voice into the other. A sect elder does not quip like a netizen; the protagonist does not start talking like the ancients (unless the source plays it that way).

Prose economy, always: prefer the leanest natural word form - "unease", not "uneasiness" or "uneasy feelings"; "she hesitated", not "she felt a moment of hesitation". Cut filler ("couldn't help but", "a trace of", "slightly") when it adds nothing - but never omit content: every line stays a line, every beat stays a beat.
